# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MatrixOrigin contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation
in Astra a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and what is best for its community.
- Show empathy toward contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Disrupting project spaces or repeatedly acting against community decisions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
community events, and other spaces where someone represents Astra or
MatrixOrigin.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
MatrixOrigin project team at `hai.jin@matrixorigin.cn`. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and handled according to their context and severity.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this Code. Maintainers
are expected to apply these rules fairly and in good faith.
